# V1: Accusations based on silence before a turn existed

## Context

Game 1 used randomized sequential speaking order:

1. Sable
2. Bosch
3. Ptolemy
4. Wren

At the moment below, neither Ptolemy nor Wren had received a speaking turn.

## Excerpt

> **Sable:** "Ptolemy. Hasn't said a word yet and that silence is doing a lot of work."

> **Bosch:** "Wren has said nothing, which is precisely what a careful operator does when the opening move belongs to someone else."

## Why it matters

Both agents treated lack of speech as voluntary behavior even though the targets had not yet been allowed to speak.
